Our Services

Flooring

The right floor sets the tone for a space and has to stand up to how it is actually used. We supply and install tile, hardwood, laminate and luxury vinyl for homes and commercial spaces across the GTA and Niagara. Everything is custom: you choose the material, colour, pattern and finish.

Every project starts with an assessment of the existing floor and subfloor, so levelling, moisture and traffic are dealt with before anything is laid. Installation includes removal of old flooring, subfloor preparation, careful layout, transitions and trims, and a clean finish.

  • Tile: porcelain, ceramic and stone for kitchens, bathrooms, entries and commercial spaces.
  • Hardwood: solid and engineered planks with a refined, lasting finish.
  • Laminate: a durable, budget-friendly look of wood or stone.
  • Vinyl: waterproof luxury vinyl plank and tile for busy homes, basements and businesses.

Custom Epoxy Floors

Our epoxy flake systems turn worn, stained or dusty concrete into a seamless, glossy surface that is easy to clean and built for hard use. We offer decorative flake and metallic systems, and every floor is custom: you choose the colours, the blend and the finish.

The process starts with diamond grinding and repair of cracks and joints so the coating bonds properly. A base coat is applied, your chosen flake or metallic finish is applied, then the surface is finished with a clear protective topcoat that adds depth, shine and resistance to stains and wear.

  • Flake systems: textured, decorative and durable, in the colour blend you choose.
  • Metallic systems: dramatic, swirling, high-gloss finishes that are unique to every floor.
  • Best for: garages, basements, shops, showrooms, warehouses and commercial kitchens.

Commercial Floor Maintenance

Floors in busy commercial spaces take a daily beating. Our maintenance programs protect your investment and keep your space clean, safe and presentable for customers and staff.

Every program is custom: we build the schedule and scope around your space and your hours so there is no disruption to your business, whether you need a one-time restoration or ongoing daily, weekly or monthly service.

  • Deep cleaning and scrubbing: machine scrubbing for tile, concrete, vinyl and epoxy floors.
  • Strip, seal and refinish: restore shine and protection to tired floors.
  • Polishing and restoration: bring dull concrete, stone and wood back to life.
  • Repairs and recoating: fix cracks, joints and chips, and refresh worn epoxy topcoats.
